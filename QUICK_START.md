# Quick Start Guide for Jacob

Hey Jacob! Your meal planner is ready to go. Here's everything you need to know:

## 🌐 Accessing Your Planner

Once GitHub Pages is enabled, go to:
**https://estradachristopher32-boop.github.io/Jacob-Planner/**

Save this URL or bookmark it!

## 🍽️ Adding Meals

1. Click **"➕ Add Meal"**
2. Fill in:
   - Meal name (e.g., "Chicken & Rice Power Bowl")
   - Day of the week
   - Meal type (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snack)
   - Calories (e.g., 850)
   - Macros: Protein, Carbs, Fats (in grams)
   - Ingredients (one per line, optional but helpful!)
3. Click **"Add Meal"**

Your meal will appear on the main screen with all the details!

## 🛒 Creating Grocery Lists

1. Add all your meals for the week first
2. Click **"🛒 Generate List"**
3. Your grocery list is automatically created!
   - Items are organized by category (Proteins, Grains, Vegetables, etc.)
   - Check off items as you shop
   - All checkmarks save automatically

## 📊 Tracking Calories

1. Click **"📊 Log Calories"**
2. The date will be pre-filled with today
3. Enter your total calories for the day
4. Add notes if you want (optional) - like "Felt great!" or "Low energy"
5. Click **"Log Entry"**

You can see your logged days in the Calorie Log tab!

## 💡 Pro Tips for Bulking

- **Calorie Target**: The app defaults to 3,500 calories/day (good for bulking!)
- **Protein Goal**: Aim for 1g per pound of body weight
  - If you weigh 180 lbs, shoot for 180g protein per day
- **Meal Prep**: 
  - Plan your whole week on Sunday
  - Cook in bulk (make 3-4 servings at once)
  - Store in containers in the fridge
- **Stay Consistent**: Log your calories every day to track progress
- **Generate Grocery List**: Do this before you shop so you don't forget anything!

## 📱 Mobile Access

The site works great on your phone! You can even add it to your home screen:

**iPhone**: Open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen
**Android**: Open in Chrome → Menu → Add to Home Screen

Then it works like a real app!

## 🔄 Resetting Data

**⚠️ CAUTION**: The "Reset All" button clears EVERYTHING (meals, grocery list, calorie logs). Only use this if you want to start completely fresh!

## ❓ Questions?

If something doesn't work or you want to add features, just ask! The app is simple HTML/CSS/JavaScript so it's easy to customize.
